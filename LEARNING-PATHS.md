Not sure where to start? Pick the path that matches your goal, then work through its topics in order. Each topic links to its section. Do the matching lab in the [Labs](Labs/README.md) folder before moving on.

## How to use these paths

- Do the lab for each topic before moving on. Reading without doing is the fastest way to forget.
- If a topic is already familiar, skim its section and do its lab. That's enough to confirm you know it.
- Revisit the [Troubleshooting log](sections/13-troubleshooting-log.md) whenever a command fails. Most real problems are already written up there.
- Once you finish a path, pick another one. Many topics appear in more than one path, so the second one goes faster.
