# 6. Dates and calendars

## Quick reference

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `date` | Shows or sets the date and time |
| `cal` | Prints a monthly or yearly calendar |
| `ncal` | Prints a calendar with days of the week down the side |
| `touch` | Changes a file's timestamps (go check section 01) |

## Dates and calendars

| Command | What it does | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `date` | Shows today's date and the time | `date` |
| `cal` | Shows this month's calendar | `cal` |
| `ncal` | Shows it with the days of the week down the side | `ncal` |
| `cal <year>` | Shows a whole year | `cal 2027` |
| `cal <month> <year>` | Shows one month | `cal 1 2027` |

Related commands that measure time, such as `time` and `sleep`, are in section 4.
